Delete your Keyro account

Keyro Invoicing by Julee Interactive Pty Ltd

You can delete your Keyro Invoicing account and its data at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and can't be undone, so you may want to export a backup first (Settings → Export Full Backup).

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Keyro and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. Scroll down to Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, or don't have the app any more, email adminkeyro@juleeinteractive.com.au from the email address you use for Keyro, with the subject “Delete my Keyro account”. We'll confirm it's you and delete your account within 7 days, and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your business details, logo and settings.
  • All your quotes, variations, invoices, schedules and client details, including client signatures.
  • Client links stop working, so your clients can no longer view your documents.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Copies in our service providers' backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Keyro's records of emails sent for you are deleted with your account. Our email provider keeps its own delivery logs for a short time before deleting them.
  • If you paid for a subscription through the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google keep their own purchase records. Deleting your Keyro account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Copies of PDFs or emails you've already sent to clients stay with them.

Questions? Email adminkeyro@juleeinteractive.com.au. See also our Privacy Policy.
