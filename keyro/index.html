Questions

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. Create an account and use Keyro free for 30 days. You only subscribe if you want to keep going.

Does it work in New Zealand?

Yes. Keyro supports Australian ABN and BAS quarters, and New Zealand NZBN and GST periods.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes. Use the iPhone or Android app on site, and sign in at keyro.juleeinteractive.com.au on your computer.

How do I cancel?

If you subscribed in the App Store or Google Play, cancel in your phone's subscription settings. If you subscribed on the web, use the manage link in your subscription receipt email. Need help? Visit Support.
