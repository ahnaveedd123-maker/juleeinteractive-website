Privacy Policy

Keyro Invoicing · Last updated 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Julee Interactive Pty Ltd (we, us) handles personal information in the Keyro Invoicing website and mobile apps (Keyro). We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, for users in New Zealand, the Information Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 2020 (NZ).

1. Who Keyro is for

Keyro is a quoting, invoicing and job scheduling app for trades and service businesses in Australia and New Zealand. In this policy, you means the business owner or staff member who has a Keyro account. Your clients are the customers you send quotes and invoices to.

2. Information we collect

From you

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely, we never see your password).
  • Business details: business name, ABN or NZBN, GST number, licence number, phone, email, address, logo, bank details for payments, trade and settings.
  • The quotes, variations, invoices, job schedules and notes you create.
  • Messages you send us for support.

About your clients (entered by you)

  • Client names, email addresses, phone numbers and job addresses.
  • When a client accepts a quote online: their typed name, drawn signature and the date and time they signed.
  • Replies your clients send to emails from Keyro, which we pass on to you.

We hold your clients' information on your behalf, to run Keyro for you. You are responsible for having your clients' permission to use their details and for telling them how you use them.

Collected automatically

  • Basic technical information needed to run and secure Keyro, such as sign-in times, device and browser type, and error logs.
  • Records of emails Keyro sends for you (who it went to, when, and whether it was delivered), so we can show you what was sent and prevent misuse.

Keyro does not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To provide Keyro: saving and syncing your documents, creating PDFs, emailing quotes, invoices and reminders to your clients, and showing clients their documents through a secure link and PIN.
  • To keep Keyro secure, prevent fraud and spam, and fix problems.
  • To provide support and tell you about your account, billing and important changes.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We only use your clients' information to provide Keyro to you. We don't contact your clients for our own purposes.

4. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run Keyro. They may only use the information to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Vercel: website and app hosting.
  • Resend: sending and receiving Keyro emails.
  • Apple and Google: if you download Keyro or pay for a subscription through the App Store or Google Play. They handle payment details under their own privacy policies; we don't see your full card number.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or with your permission.

5. Information stored outside Australia and New Zealand

Some of our service providers store or process information overseas, including in Japan (email delivery) and the United States (hosting). We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it in line with Australian and New Zealand privacy law.

6. Security

Information is encrypted when it travels over the internet. Each account can only see its own data, client links are protected by a private link and a PIN, and access to our systems is restricted. No online service can be completely secure, so we also recommend exporting a backup from Settings from time to time.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your account and documents are deleted from our live systems straight away, and from our providers' backups within 30 days. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

Your clients' information is kept as part of your documents, and is deleted when you delete those documents or your account.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change most of your information in Keyro at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing adminkeyro@juleeinteractive.com.au.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to. See Delete your Keyro account.

If you are a client of a business that uses Keyro and want to access or correct your details, please contact that business first. You can also email us and we'll help.

9. Children

Keyro is a business tool for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

10. Complaints

If you have a privacy question or complaint, email adminkeyro@juleeinteractive.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or, in New Zealand, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make an important change, we'll tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
