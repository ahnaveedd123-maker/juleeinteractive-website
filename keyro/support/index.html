Common questions

I forgot my password

On the Keyro sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new password. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

My client can't open the quote or invoice

Clients open documents with the link in your email or message, plus the 4-digit PIN shown in the same message. Make sure the document was saved before you shared it (you'll see it in your Quotes list), then send it again from Share.

My email went to the client's spam folder

Keyro emails are fully authenticated, but some inboxes are cautious with new senders. Ask your client to mark the email as Not spam or add your Keyro address to their contacts. Future emails will then arrive normally.

Where do my clients' replies go?

When a client replies to a Keyro email, Keyro forwards the reply to the business email in your Settings. Just hit Reply to answer them from your own inbox.

I'm not registered for GST

When making a quote, untick Charge GST above the totals. No GST is added, and invoices say “Invoice” instead of “Tax Invoice”. Keyro remembers your choice for new quotes.

How do I mark an invoice as paid?

Tap Mark paid next to the invoice on the Home screen (Needs Attention) or in your Quotes list. The balance on the original quote updates automatically.

Can I use Keyro without internet?

Yes. You can create and edit documents offline. They sync to your account the next time you're online. Sharing and emailing need an internet connection.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export Full Backup to download a copy of all your documents.
